# Youth refusal and boredom reactions

The former teenage behavior now applies to the second stage, displayed as Youth (青年), using the approved adult angry artwork and red anger mark. Teenage images and its archived prototype remain unchanged; the active lifecycle stays child → adult → elder.

Each eligible player request has a baseline 25% refusal for food, petting, and play (toolbar or player-thrown toy). Random feeding refusal shows anger for two seconds. Angry refusal of petting triggers the original quick escape hop; angry refusal of play approaches the toy, prepares a kick and applies the stronger impulse only when touching the ball. Picking up the toy cancels the kick. Refusals give no need/growth rewards and charge no play energy. Angry refusals reduce mood by 5 once per request.

The first eligible request is not counted as a repeat. Repeating it one or two more times becomes boring (the second or third total attempt, chosen randomly). These include refusals; unclickable/blocked actions do not count. After the full-need and random-anger checks, a boring repeat uses its approved unimpressed face and refuses, staying completely still, with no escape hop or kick. Each unimpressed refusal reduces mood by 3 once. The streak remains until a different care action, successful cleaning, or a gap of eight active gameplay seconds. Toolbar play and throwing the physical ball share the play streak. Automatic toy demonstrations/invitations do not count. Critical-mood comfort always succeeds and clears petting repetition. Sleep, death, growth and restarting clear repetition too.

Full corresponding bars also cause a stationary unimpressed refusal: hunger for feeding, mood for petting or playing (including the physical toy), and energy for a player-requested sleep. Rounded values of 100 (actual values ≥99.5) count as full so one frame of passive drain does not bypass the rule. An automatic low-energy nap never triggers this refusal. Priority is critical-mood comfort, then full-need refusal, then random angry refusal, then repetitive refusal, then acceptance. Fullness, anger and repetition never stack penalties. A full-need refusal costs 3 mood; otherwise random anger remains available even for repeated play. Anger only appears during a timed refusal/reaction; mood 30–49 no longer fixes the angry face in place. Below 30 mood the existing crying state still applies. Passive artwork never charges interaction penalties each frame.

The public name is Youth in the guide, growth announcement and preview title. The internal `adult` key and original asset filenames remain for compatibility. `/previews/youth/` is the preferred preview; existing `/previews/adult/` links continue to work.

Accepted play keeps its current running approach, gentle tap, reward cooldown and energy costs (32 for toolbar play, 5 per rewarded physical contact). Adult automatic toy invitations remain available outside anger. Critical-mood petting is always accepted; stinky-waste crying and the hungry pose retain priority. Children and seniors do not roll for refusal.

Help freezes reaction time and the angry expression. Sleep, death, growth to senior, and restarting clear the refusal state. Reactions delay new waste creation until finished, without changing existing piles' stink/fatal timers. Natural lifespan, letter/envelope, rebirth, stage sizes and source PNGs are unchanged.

To review, open `previews/youth/` and offer food, strokes and play a few times. Some attempts should show anger, hop away or kick the toy away; accepted attempts should still work. Try comforting a critically sad adult, picking up a toy during a refusal, and opening/closing the guide mid-reaction. Check the younger and senior previews for their unchanged behavior.
