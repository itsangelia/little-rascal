# Adult refusal reactions

The former teenage behavior now applies to the adult stage, using the approved adult angry artwork and red anger mark. Teenage images and its archived prototype remain unchanged; the active lifecycle stays child → adult → elder.

Each eligible player request rolls independently: 25% refusal for food, 20% for petting, and 20% for play (toolbar or player-thrown toy). Feeding refusal shows anger for two seconds. Rejected petting triggers the original quick escape hop; rejected play approaches the toy, prepares a kick and applies the stronger impulse only when touching the ball. Picking up the toy cancels the kick. Refusals give no need/growth rewards and charge no play energy.

Accepted play keeps its current running approach, gentle tap, reward cooldown and energy costs (32 for toolbar play, 5 per rewarded physical contact). Adult automatic toy invitations remain available outside anger. Critical-mood petting is always accepted; stinky-waste crying and the hungry pose retain priority. Children and seniors do not roll for refusal.

Help freezes reaction time and the angry expression. Sleep, death, growth to senior, and restarting clear the refusal state. Reactions delay new waste creation until finished, without changing existing piles' stink/fatal timers. Natural lifespan, letter/envelope, rebirth, stage sizes and source PNGs are unchanged.

To review, open `previews/adult/` and offer food, strokes and play a few times. Some attempts should show anger, hop away or kick the toy away; accepted attempts should still work. Try comforting a critically sad adult, picking up a toy during a refusal, and opening/closing the guide mid-reaction. Check the younger and senior previews for their unchanged behavior.
